About Me

I'm a GIS Professional with over three and a half years of experience and graduate training in Geographic Information Science & Technology from UMass Amherst. My work has supported municipal utility, transportation planning, nonprofit initiatives, and environmental stewardship, where I've applied GIS to make data more accurate, accessible and actionable.

Along the way I have:

  • Cleaned up legacy data and spearheaded new data collection initiatives
  • Digitized & related historic records to streamline information retrieval
  • Developed responsive dashboards balancing flexibility, efficiency, and usability
  • Collaborated with diverse teams to introduce & expand GIS capacity
  • Adapted to new tools and established worksflows quickly

Explore the map to see how I've contributed to projects across organizations!

Outside of formal projects, I enjoy exploring how GIS can enrich my own everyday experiences. In particular, I've created offline maps visualizing my CD collection or plants that I've foraged, using open-source tools like Leaflet and OpenStreetMap. GIS isn't only a professional skill — it's also a personal passion that shapes how I see and experience the world around me.
